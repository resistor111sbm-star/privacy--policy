Privacy Policy for Balance IQ

Transparent guidelines on how we protect, store, and manage your financial records with privacy-first standards.

🗓️ Effective Date: October 20, 2026

Welcome to Balance IQ ("we", "our", or "us"). We deeply value your trust and are staunchly committed to protecting your personal data and digital privacy. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines transparently how our application collects, utilizes, manages, and secures your information when you engage with our financial tracking services.

1. Information Architecture & Data Collection

Balance IQ is engineered with a privacy-first mindset. To ensure optimal performance regarding income analytics, expense logging, and report compilation, we interact with the following categories of data:

  • User-Provided Financial Records: Income values, expense figures, custom category tags, budget notes, and monetary transactions that you manually input into the application database.
  • Generated Documents: Exported financial reports formatted in PDF or CSV structures compiled directly from your local inputs.
  • Telemetry & Device Diagnostics: Non-identifying device metrics, including operating system versions, crash logs, and performance diagnostics strictly utilized to eliminate bugs and enhance application stability.
2. Purpose and Utilization of Data

The information we process is strictly compartmentalized to deliver and optimize core product dynamics:

  • To dynamically compute net balances, categorize expenditures, and generate intuitive trend analytics.
  • To construct secure, downloadable financial summaries in structural PDF and CSV formats upon user request.
  • To continuously optimize app responsiveness, fix critical bugs, and refine overall User Interface/User Experience (UI/UX) dynamics.
3. Data Governance, Storage & Security Protocols
  • Local & Secured State Management: Your financial transaction logs are processed and secured within robust storage environments to guarantee uninterrupted, private access throughout your user sessions.
  • Proactive Security Measures: We deploy industry-standard administrative, physical, and technical safeguards designed meticulously to mitigate unauthorized entry, data leakage, alteration, or unlawful destruction.
4. Zero Third-Party Data Monetization
  • We maintain a strict Zero-Sale Policy. We do not trade, rent, lease, or monetize your personal or financial database to external commercial entities or advertisers.
  • Data interaction is strictly confined to internal operational frameworks required to maintain app features or satisfy mandatory regulatory compliance.
5. User Autonomy, Rights & Control Dashboard
  • Autonomous Modification: You maintain absolute administrative rights to input, edit, override, or permanently purge any transaction logs or categories inside Balance IQ instantly.
  • Portability Control: You hold the absolute right to extract and download your complete financial records into portable CSV or PDF formats whenever required.
6. Policy Modifications & Evolution

We reserve the authority to update, amend, or revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological upgrades or legal changes. Significant modifications will be instantly communicated via our official update channels, and the "Effective Date" at the top will be appropriately refreshed.
